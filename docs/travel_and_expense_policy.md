# Travel & Expense (T&E) Policy

This policy governs business travel, meals, and related spending. All amounts are in USD.

## Meals

**Client meals.** Meals with clients or prospects are capped at **$75 per attendee**. Any single client meal with a total above **$500** requires **VP pre-approval** before the expense is incurred. Client meals submitted without required pre-approval are held as pending until reviewed.

**Team meals.** Internal team meals are capped at **$40 per attendee**.

Alcohol may not exceed 20% of a meal's total bill and is never reimbursable for solo meals.

## Lodging

Hotel lodging is capped at **$300 per night**. Nightly rates above this cap are flagged and require justification. Rates at or below the cap need no extra approval.

## Airfare

Only **economy class** airfare is reimbursable by default. **Business or first class requires VP approval** and is otherwise held as pending.

## Ground transport

Taxis, rideshare, and transit for business purposes are reimbursable at actual cost. No fixed cap, but expenses should be reasonable.

## Approval thresholds

- Software purchases above **$1,000** require **manager approval**.
- Conference or event spending above **$2,000** requires **director approval**.
- Any single expense above **$2,500** requires **Finance Director sign-off**, regardless of category.

Expenses awaiting a required approval are recorded with status `pending`. Expenses that breach a hard cap are recorded as `flagged`.
