# Expense Categories

Definitions for each category used in the expense system. Use these to classify spending consistently.

- **Client Meals** — food or drink where a client or prospect is present. Subject to the $75-per-attendee cap and the $500 VP pre-approval threshold.
- **Team Meals** — internal meals among employees only. Subject to the $40-per-attendee cap.
- **Lodging** — hotel or short-term stays for business travel. Subject to the $300-per-night cap.
- **Airfare** — flights for business travel. Economy is reimbursable by default; business or first class needs VP approval.
- **Transport** — ground transport such as taxis, rideshare, transit, and parking.
- **Software** — tools, licenses, and subscriptions used for work. Purchases above $1,000 need manager approval.
- **Conference** — registration and fees for conferences and events. Spending above $2,000 needs director approval.
- **Office Supplies** — physical goods for the office or team, such as stationery and branded items.
