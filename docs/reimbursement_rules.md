# Reimbursement Rules

How expenses are submitted, reviewed, and reimbursed.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses within **30 days** of the transaction date. Each expense must include a receipt for any amount above **$25**. Expenses missing a required receipt are held until the receipt is provided.

## Reimbursable vs. non-reimbursable

**Reimbursable:** business travel, client and team meals within policy caps, lodging within the nightly cap, economy airfare, conference registration, software and subscriptions used for work, and business ground transport.

**Not reimbursable:** personal entertainment, spouse or family travel, airline seat upgrades beyond economy without VP approval, alcohol beyond 20% of a meal bill, and late fees or fines.

## Status meanings

- **approved** — reviewed and cleared for reimbursement.
- **pending** — awaiting a required approval or missing information (for example, a client meal over $500 without VP pre-approval, or business-class airfare).
- **flagged** — breaches a hard cap in the T&E policy (for example, lodging above $300 per night) and needs correction or written justification.

## Timeline

Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle. Pending or flagged expenses are not reimbursed until resolved.
